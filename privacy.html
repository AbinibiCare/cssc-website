Information may be used to respond to your enquiry, understand your requirements, prepare a proposal, provide requested services and maintain appropriate business records.

CSSC does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate the business or deliver an agreed service, subject to appropriate confidentiality and legal obligations.
