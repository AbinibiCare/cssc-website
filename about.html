Our Lead Consultant

Mathew Adewale

Lead Consultant — Finance, Project Controls, Risk & Business Systems

Mathew Adewale is a multidisciplinary finance, project controls and risk professional with more than 20 years of experience across infrastructure, logistics, energy, manufacturing, banking, technology and complex business operations.

His experience spans financial leadership, project and portfolio controls, risk management, performance reporting, governance, process improvement and business systems. He brings these disciplines together to help organisations improve visibility, strengthen controls and make confident, evidence-led decisions.

Mathew’s professional experience includes managing complex project portfolios, developing project-control and governance frameworks, improving working-capital requirements, strengthening management reporting and significantly reducing month-end reporting timelines across multiple entities.

As CSSC’s Lead Consultant, Mathew provides senior oversight of client engagements and coordinates the specialist expertise needed for each assignment. His approach is practical, collaborative and focused on delivering measurable improvements that clients can sustain.
